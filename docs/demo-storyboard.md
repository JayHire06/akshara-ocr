# Akshara OCR - Demo Video Storyboard

**Video Length:** ~60 seconds
**Format:** 16:9 Landscape & 9:16 Portrait (for social)
**Style:** Fast-paced, sleek, high-contrast, showing real UI interaction

## Scene 1: The Hook (0s - 10s)
* **Visual:** Pitch black background. Suddenly, a glowing lime green (#c6f135) line sweeps across the screen like a scanner. It reveals a crumpled, dusty old document in a complex Indian script (e.g., old Devanagari manuscript).
* **Text Overlay:** "Reading ancient scripts used to be impossible."
* **Transition:** The document minimizes and snaps into the center of the **Akshara OCR Upload Screen**.

## Scene 2: The Magic (10s - 25s)
* **Visual:** The mouse cursor (sleek, high-res) drags the file into the dashed Drop Zone of the Akshara OCR interface.
* **Action:** The cursor clicks the 'Detect Language' dropdown (showing the 11 beautiful native script options) and selects Hindi. It hits the **Extract Text** button.
* **UI Focus:** Zoom in on the minimal, glowing spinner during the `Processing` state. Text: "Analyzing Document...".
* **Audio/Pacing:** Fast heartbeat/ticking sound, building tension.

## Scene 3: The Reveal (25s - 40s)
* **Visual:** BAM! The Results screen loads instantly in the 50/50 split view.
* **Action:** The camera pans from left (the messy image crop) to the right (perfectly formatted, clean Noto Sans Devanagari digital text).
* **UI Focus:** Callout bubble pops up next to the Confidence Badge showing `98% Accuracy`.
* **Action:** Cursor clicks the "Copy" button. A slick green `#10b981` success toast slides in.

## Scene 4: Versatility (40s - 50s)
* **Visual:** A rapid-fire montage of different documents being dropped into the app:
  - A blurry street sign in Tamil -> Extracted instantly.
  - A handwritten note in Bengali -> Extracted perfectly.
  - A printed newspaper in Telugu -> Cleanly digitized.
* **Text Overlay:** "11 Scripts. 100% Instant."

## Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action (50s - 60s)
* **Visual:** The screen fades to the dark background (`#0f1115`). The Akshara OCR logo (the 'अ' scanner mark) draws itself in glowing lime green.
* **Text Overlay:** "Akshara OCR. Digitize India's Heritage."
* **Action:** A button reading "Try it for free at akshara.ai" pulses on screen.
* **Audio:** Deep bass drop, resolving into a lingering crisp chime.
